Many questions in public health are causal. Would this treatment delay dementia onset? How much of a gap in cancer outcomes between groups could a policy close? We build methods that answer these questions under clearly stated assumptions.

Our work looks at effects beyond the average, such as effects on quantiles of survival time, and at causal decomposition of disparities into the parts that are open to intervention.
