Good applied work needs two things: a clear scientific question and a method that fits the data. We work closely with researchers in cancer epidemiology, nutrition, and aging, many of them at the National Cancer Institute (NIH).

Many of these projects use quantile tools. A biomarker or a diet can matter most for people at the high or low end, even when the average barely moves.
